The problem

Stencils are scattered and inconsistent

The Azure stencils you can find are a patchwork: hundreds of products spread across many separate stencils from different providers, often mixing raster PNG icons with vectors, dropped at wildly different sizes, with no connection points or shape data, and many already out of date. Pulling a clean diagram together means hunting across sets that never quite match.

The solution

One unified, standardised set

Visio-Azure unifies and standardises the lot: 1,764 masters across 17 groups, every one a clean vector normalised to the same size, with connection points, shape data, and working search, generated programmatically and verified against a per-master contract on every build.

Every master,
fully loaded.

The same contract applied to all 1,764 icons, automatically.

search

Search that works

Keywords written into all four cells Visio's indexer reads, shaped by an empirical lab against Microsoft's own stencils. Type "key vault" and find it.

hub

Nine connection points

North, East, South, West, four corners, and SouthOfText on every icon. Connectors glue to anchors, not to bounding boxes.

database

Resource shape data

ResourceId, Location, ResourceName, ResourceGroupName, ResourceType, TagsTable, SubscriptionId on every master. Populate from ARM exports, tags, or a CMDB.

dark_mode

Dark-mode twins

Twenty-seven near-black logos ship a white -DM variant with white captions, so dark-canvas designs stay legible.
