# Premium

The Visio stencils in this project are **free and GPL-licensed** -- all 1,773 masters, both unit systems, forever. **Premium** *(in build)* is the layer on top: the assets and styles that take real, ongoing effort to produce and are easy for others to repackage.

## What Premium includes

- 🎨 **Styled variants** -- alternate visual treatments of the full set (glass / 3D / mono and more) for design-led decks and diagrams.
- 📐 **Per-icon SVG files** -- normalised, cleaned, drop-ready for any tool.
- 🖼️ **Per-icon PNG renders** -- 256 px and 1024 px sets for slides, docs, and the web.
- 🚀 **Early access** to new releases and new styled sets.

If all you need is Visio, you never have to pay anything -- the stencils are complete on their own. Premium is for people who consume the icons as standalone files outside Visio, or who want the styled looks.

## Why it's set up this way

This isn't paywalling for its own sake -- it's a direct response to how open assets get treated.

Earlier free distribution of the loose `.svg` / `.png` files saw them **scraped, re-hosted on third-party asset sites, and resold inside commercial products with the licence and attribution stripped**. The licence asked for one thing -- acknowledge the source -- and that was repeatedly ignored. Freely distributing the raw, trivially-repackaged files effectively meant handing the work to people who'd take credit and money for it.

So the line is drawn deliberately:

- **Free and open:** the functional `.vssx` stencils -- the part the community actually needs to work in Visio.
- **Premium:** the raw asset files and the styled variants -- the part that's easy to lift and resell.

That keeps the useful thing free while keeping the project funded enough to stay maintained and regenerated as Microsoft's icons evolve.

## Licence on the asset files

The SVG and PNG files are the same icons rendered to standalone formats; the licence is unchanged -- **GPL-3.0** for the packaging work, plus the Microsoft Azure icon usage terms (free for use in diagrams; attribution to Microsoft for the icon design). Premium gates **distribution access**, not a different licence. You can use the assets commercially provided you keep attribution to both Microsoft (icon design) and this project (the normalisation / packaging / styling work).
